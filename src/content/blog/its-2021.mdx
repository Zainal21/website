The year 2020 was only a few hours away from ending when I sat down to write this. It felt like a good moment to stop, look back at what the year gave me, and decide what 2021 was going to be about.

So, a recap — and then the targets. Let's go.

## What 2020 actually gave me

### Events

The pandemic took away venues, but it never took away enthusiasm. In **October**, my team and I jumped into a slightly spontaneous project to take part in **Hacktoberfest 2020** — our first real taste of contributing to open source under pressure.

I also started plugging into the wider community through online events across Indonesia, like the **Deeptech meetups** and the online sessions hosted by the **SurabayaJS community**. Even from home, I found that showing up to these things teaches you more than an hour of tutorials ever could — because you get to hear how other people actually think about problems.

### Side projects

Side projects and helping friends with their coursework turned out to be one of the best ways to learn. Working with other people forces you to get *used* to collaboration: explaining your code, reviewing theirs, dividing work, and hiding your frustration when the merge conflicts pile up.

It's always worth learning to build together. Teamwork is a skill, not a personality trait — and I was glad to start practicing it early.

### Sports

Even in a year of lockdowns, football stayed. I kept training with **Merpati FC**, and we were preparing for the **Pordes 2020** event in August when it happened: we took **3rd place** and finished as the **overall champion** of the tournament, winning across several categories.

Alhamdulillah — a trophy made the year feel a little less lost than the news made it look.

### School

School stayed online, and online learning brings its own kind of laziness. But it had a silver lining: with no commute and fewer distractions, it gave me more time to explore new things — and I used most of it exploring the world of web development.

The clock is ticking though. Graduation was in sight, and I knew the timer on "student with no responsibilities" was almost out.

## Targets for 2021

Let me be honest: many goals from 2020 stayed unfinished, and I didn't have a clear list for the new year yet when I wrote this. But here's the plan I committed to — with maximum effort:

- **Learn React, Vue.js, and Nuxt.js** — modern frontend, properly.
- **Deepen my knowledge of Laravel and Node.js** — the backend where I already felt at home.
- **Learn Design Patterns** — because writing code that works and writing code that survives are two different skills.
- **Read books, regularly** — not just documentation.
- **Contribute actively to open source** — not just during Hacktoberfest.

…and a hundred smaller things that will inevitably appear along the way.

Let's hope I can actually achieve all of this. Aamiin. 😄

Here's to a year of building, contributing, and staying curious.
